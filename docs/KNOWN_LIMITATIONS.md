# Known limitations

- The extracted source began at 4/10 and reached only 5/10 after the one permitted targeted visual pass; it did not meet the 6/10 precondition for a worn standalone Quest gate.
- Cockpit readability and macro/water variation improved, but far terrain, runway/airport presentation, ground materials, and reservoir rendering remain visibly synthetic.
- Worn stereo stability, seat placement, comfort, and sustained 72 Hz performance were not tested because the machine visual gate stopped progression first.
- The Unity prototype backend is authoritative and is not a certified or final C172 flight model.
- Native JSBSim is excluded because its unchanged promotion gate passed only 10/11 scenarios.
- The KOG_THORNS aircraft is redistributed under CC BY 4.0 with attribution; CC BY does not grant Cessna/Textron trademark rights, so public commercial release still requires a branding/no-endorsement review.
- Several cockpit controls lack separable pivots and remain static.
- Compatibility beyond the tested Quest 3/Unity configuration is not claimed.
- The application is not FAA approved, not for navigation, and provides no legal training credit.

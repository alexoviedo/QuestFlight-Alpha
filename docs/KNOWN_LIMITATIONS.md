# Known limitations

- **Worn Visual Runtime V1 is a machine-validated merge candidate awaiting its exact-APK worn review.** The candidate replaces 42 repeated grove cues with 946 semantically aligned, DTM-grounded groves (52,976 triangles in four opaque draws), preserves all 9,692 OSM-footprint building shells while adding physically scaled semantic facade rules, enables the authoritative cabin-glass response from the seated view, and adds one layer-isolated 512-resolution cockpit shadow sun. EditMode 115/115, PlayMode 9/9, 81-view Visual QA, product integrity, dependency closure, the static Quest planning gate, live simulator stereo, and Android build pass. The exact APK is 134,060,510 bytes with SHA-256 `eb9499403126e4820ca0e42130ee1bddd84ff35c5b6909c9eb7184062ec431e8`; no worn score or physical timing is attributed until that binary is tested.
- The new groves are deterministic aggregate scenery derived from the aligned public-domain imagery/canopy/DTM evidence, not a surveyed botanical inventory. They substantially improve coverage and height at pattern altitude, but the low-cost multi-crown silhouettes remain stylized and can look faceted at very close range; species, trunks, season, and individual crown form are approximate. The 82.576% coverage figure applies only to eligible represented canopy pixels inside the bounded 0–1,500 m authoring zone.
- Context buildings remain footprint-preserving procedural shells rather than reference-authored architecture. Their facade modules, entrances, opaque window reflections, category palettes, and metre-scale UVs are deterministic and semantically coherent, but individual window/door placement and material identity are inferred from incomplete OSM tags and are not surveyed. Hero-building quality and literal close-range photorealism remain future work.
- Cabin glass uses a bounded one-pass static ground/sky reflection approximation, and cockpit depth uses two opaque casters with a layer-isolated hard-shadow sun. These avoid realtime probes, extra cameras, cascades, transparency growth, and broad aircraft shadows, but the exact worn build must still prove that glazing is visible from the seat and that cockpit shadows are stable and comfortable. Instruments remain mostly static or blank; functional data binding was outside this visual milestone.

These are Editor planning values, not physical Quest timing. Meta XR live stereo passes, but all ten deterministic `.vrs` replays remain absent.

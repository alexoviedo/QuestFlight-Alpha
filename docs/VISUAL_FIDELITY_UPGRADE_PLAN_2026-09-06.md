### 2. Detect whole canopies and preserve their topology

Produce a canonical **grove map**, independent of rendering and leaf perforation:

1. Construct an initial 1 m evidence grid with classified vegetation support, above-ground height quantiles, return density and confidence. Refine boundary imagery at the existing native orthophoto scale where useful; do not call upsampled evidence new resolution. Class labels, spatial density and ground support must be checked, not blindly trusted.
2. Fuse elevated vegetation support with image texture/color and known building, water and playing-surface exclusions. Height distinguishes grass from tree foliage. Color is supporting evidence: sunlit/desaturated foliage and shaded connecting branches cannot be discarded merely because they fail the old green/brightness rule. Confirmed excluded surfaces remain excluded. Because ground RGB is no longer pasted onto crown cards, recovering real bright foliage does not restore road pixels to the foliage texture.
3. Grow components through supported canopy; bridge only small sampling gaps corroborated by imagery or vegetation returns. Keep courtyards, paths, clearings and disconnected crowns distinct. A road underneath a real overhanging crown is not automatically an empty canopy gap; require elevated vegetation evidence before accepting that connection. Keep leaf-sized transparency out of the connectivity calculation.
4. Process tiles with overlapping evidence borders and stitch labels into stable map-wide IDs. Match through real supported adjacency, not bounding-box overlap. Store each component's outer ring, interior holes, height envelope, density/confidence, source references, uncertain boundaries and associated existing trees. Preserve uncertain/no-data regions explicitly instead of reporting fabricated complete coverage.
5. Use a bounded computer-vision fallback for ambiguous image boundaries: **SAM 2.1 prompted by verified vegetation seeds and negative surface examples**, checked against height and topology. It is a class-agnostic segmentation proposal tool, not an autonomous tree detector. Do not use it to invent heights, train a new model from scratch, or force uncertain regions into canopy. Its [official repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2) documents point/box prompting, checkpoints and their Apache 2.0 license. Install/run it offline only if the source-led method needs it and its benefit justifies setup. If LiDAR is unavailable in a region, use verified imagery segmentation with explicitly estimated heights; leave unresolved regions on the current trees.

**Detection gate before geometry:** reuse existing aerial comparison locations and annotate a small fixed reference set of 30 crops: five each covering dense deciduous groves, narrow riparian/tree rows, roads/buildings, lawns/athletic fields, conifer/mixed slopes and tile-edge/uncertain areas. Keep 18 crops held out from tuning. Trace macro boundaries and real gaps independently of generated masks; attach uncertainty bands to ambiguous edges. Proposed acceptance targets, not measured results: at least **95% canopy precision and 90% recall** on unambiguous held-out pixels, **90% of boundary points within 1.5 m**, no false bridge across the fixed road/clearing examples, and identical component identity through processing seams. Report split/merge errors as well as pixel scores; a high area score cannot excuse broken groves. Do not silently relax these targets or label an old survey as ground truth for 2020 change areas.

### 3. Compose a grove volume using small foliage clusters

Author **one logical canopy volume per connected grove**, with internal lobes and openings derived from the height/density evidence. Use measured upper structure and supported vertical returns where available; estimate lower foliage bounds conservatively where LiDAR cannot resolve them and label those estimates. Ground shadows must not become foliage volume. The volume is an offline composition guide, not an opaque shell that will be rendered.

Distribute the existing small three-plane leaf clusters over the upper, outer and supported interior portions of that volume. Keep card dimensions and leaf texel density comparable to the currently acceptable individual-tree clusters. Larger grove area receives more locally sized clusters and shared texture reuse, never a stretched leaf image or a single giant X. Orient clusters from local lobes and deterministic variation, not rays from one grove center. Shape exposed edges, crown rises, valleys and openings independently of old per-tree circles. Retain sufficient top and interior coverage; a perimeter fringe alone repeats the sparse failure.

Fit the composition against fixed top, aerial oblique and low-side views: remove redundant buried clusters, preserve useful silhouette clusters and fill unsupported artificial holes only within confirmed foliage volume. Measure rendered alpha coverage and silhouette against the source-guided reference; inspect texture detail and depth, since silhouette metrics alone cannot validate realism. Use the same authored volume and nested cluster identities for reduced LODs. Lower LODs must preserve aggregate canopy coverage rather than simply deleting a fixed fraction of cards. All variations are seeded by persistent grove/lobe IDs, so head turns and page loads cannot recompose them.

Reuse project leaf textures, material lighting/fog and provenance; initially add no new leaf atlas. Sample broad, robust canopy tint from confirmed foliage only, rejecting obvious ground/shadow outliers. Do not rebake ground shadows as leaf albedo. Keep trunks/branches that actually exist in the baseline. Create explicit ownership between grove coverage and replaced tree crowns, including partial boundary overlaps; avoid duplicate crowns, trunks or unsupported empty edges. Preserve existing trees until their replacement assets are loaded, and restore them if loading fails. The former angular overlay must be disabled in the experimental reference and candidate after approval, with its source/evidence retained for comparison.

## 5. Trial gate, stop rules and complete rollout

Each distinct source/channel contract gets one initial treatment and at most one correction justified by a diagnosed failure. Do not search settings indefinitely. This is an implementation policy, not a claim that two trials guarantee a result. Reopen a rejected treatment only for a new source or new evidence, not merely stronger sharpening or blending. Working full batches may run as long as required; these limits target repeated diagnosis, not coverage.

Before the trial, record the visible defect, source reference, exact target consumers, protected channels, output budget and the view in which improvement should be visible. Use one representative and one stress case where necessary (alpha edges, smallest LOD, roughest material, tile boundary). Technical smoke must establish correctness **and** a useful visual result before automatic full-batch expansion. No human art approval between smoke and batch.

A treatment passes only when all of the following are supported:

- The same-camera A/B shows a useful source-faithful improvement at the intended flight distance and an oblique view, without relying on zoomed inspection. Crop metrics supplement inspection; a tiny image delta alone proves neither benefit nor harm.
- Android compression, mip reduction and existing LODs preserve the benefit without halos, excess darkness, shifting palette, false detail, alpha damage or seams. Offline motion sequences screen for instability; they do not prove headset shimmer/comfort acceptance.
- Protected geometry/indices, transforms, occupied attributes, GUIDs, slice IDs, pixel-grid/georeferencing and masks pass exact checks. Only declared output channels may differ. Establish any decoded-image tolerances from unchanged control imports before the batch; never loosen exact invariants.
- Resource deltas are known. No additional samples, passes, materials, draws, transparent slots, texture dimensions/array depth, mip residency or vertex-stream bytes by default. Existing traffic shader work is a conditional exception, not a free-cost precedent. Further shader extensions require a compelling demonstrated result and measured static cost within existing limits, followed by final device review.

On success, expand immediately through the entire compatible batch, preserving source/recipe identity. On failure, retain originals and continue other families. A missing input, unreadable contract or unverified consumer is **blocked**, not an artistic rejection. A deadline is never evidence that an asset is ineligible.

Keep backend/model/source/settings/output hashes, actual CPU/GPU execution and cache identity. Reuse [tools/offline_scenery_remaster.py](../tools/offline_scenery_remaster.py) and authoritative authoring tools; extend only missing operations necessary for this candidate. No new dashboard, generic asset framework or inference-stack replacement. Neural processing must not alter masks, heights or packed PBR data as if they were photos.

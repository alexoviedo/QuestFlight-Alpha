# SGS 2-33 visual and control reference repair, 2026-10-15

The downloaded Sketchfab donor was inspected and retained unchanged. The user rejected its silhouette and the first repair. Its wings, tail, canopy and body proved unsuitable for the requested geometry gate, so the current airframe is an original loft reconstruction against a dimensioned three-view. Reference drawings and generated assets remain local and uncommitted; distribution clearance is not established by inspection. The editable Blender derivative and reference receipts are outside the repository in `T2-QuestFlight-product-artifacts/sgs_visual_repair_20261015`.

## Implementation choices

The complete airframe was reconstructed with a closed nose/fuselage, a front canopy ending at a sloping frame, fixed rear glazing, dimensioned wing taper, dihedral and washout, shaped ailerons and tail surfaces, round coaming, arched panel, seats, pedals and original gauge geometry. Split normals are now exported and retained; the old triangle-by-triangle normal recalculation caused much of the faceted appearance. A small SGS-only surface shader uses vertex color, ambient/directional lighting and bounded satin highlights. It adds no textures, reflection camera or global rendering changes.

The initial reference fit used separate blue brake and green bungee-style trim levers on the left and a red release below the panel. The blue brake stalk and its rotary interaction were superseded by the combined brake handle correction below. This is not the later trim-at-stick variant. Native gauges retain their existing software quantities and a 1013.25 hPa reference setting; they are not copies of a specific operator panel. No invented operating limits or operator placards were added.

Control anchor coordinates are engineering estimates in the existing CG frame. The nominal SGS seat eye and cockpit anchors were lowered0.52m into the enclosed reference cabin; the old eye sat above its appropriate position. This changes the authored seat-relative pose only. The native model, mass, contacts, tow hook and physics remain unchanged; OpenXR retains ownership of the tracked camera. Grips are evaluated against a nominal shoulder position and a 0.68 m reach envelope across travel. This establishes a geometric check only; worn reach, controller contact and comfort still require human acceptance. Brake/trim selection volumes now follow the same rotation as the visible handles, including after regrabbing. Pull distance is converted through the inverse grip arc. Release remains a guarded one-shot input through the existing SDK and native control path.

## Frozen scope and acceptance boundaries

No increase to the existing ceiling of 8,000 SGS triangles, three renderers, two materials and zero new textures. No scenery/quality reduction or performance optimization is used to pay for this repair. All product objects remain authored and serialized. This is a reference visual improvement, not a photorealistic or measured replica claim. Standard scene resource failures, actual Quest timing, aircraft fidelity and human acceptance remain separate evidence categories.


## Dimensioned three-view correction

The final geometry reference is [Martin Simons1996 three-view from Schweizer Aircraft Corporation supplied information](https://rclibrary.co.uk/3view.asp?ID=16826), preserved with its credits in the external two-page PDF. It specifies51ft span,60in root/30in tip chord, outboard taper,1.33deg dihedral, NACA43012A at4deg root/2.315deg mid/1deg tip incidence, and93in horizontal tail span. The scanned side elevation is normalized separately in length/height to7.8486m/2.8321m; its pixel tracing is not manufacturing CAD. The wing kink is derived from the published219.48sqft area and chord dimensions.

[UIUC Summary of Low-Speed Airfoil Data, Volume5, AppendixA p314](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/uiuc_lsat/Low-Speed-Airfoil-Data-V5.pdf) supplies the **TRUE** NACA43012A coordinates. The book explicitly distinguishes them from the inaccurate wind-tunnel specimen; that specimen's coordinates and low-Re polars are not used. Tail sections use an explicitly estimated8% symmetric profile because the inspected primary data did not identify their section designation. This provides real thickness and a rounded leading edge without claiming an undocumented exact tail airfoil.

The closed fuselage/front-canopy/rear-glazing envelope is checked independently from scenery and wings: all512 directions cast from the nominal eye intersect it. This is a geometric enclosure witness, not a leak-free manufacturing mesh or human head-clearance certification. Moving ailerons and rudder now use their authored hinge axes. The two-sided canopy uses bounded view-dependent tint/glint and the existing transparent-window background motion/depth policy; no texture or reflection camera is added.

## Balanced dive-brake correction, 2026-10-16

The SGS uses paired upper and lower balanced dive brakes on each inboard wing. The [FAA type data sheet G2EA, revision 4](https://www.soarccsc.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/G2EA_Rev-4.pdf) specifies upper travel of 85 degrees up and lower travel of 75 degrees down, with a +5 degree tolerance. The existing nominal angles were correct; the previous broad red boxes, hinge placement and lower blade direction were not.

The [Schweizer manual preflight illustration](https://www.airplanesandrockets.com/airplanes/Schweizer-2-33A-Sailplane-Manual-p1.htm), the Schweizer-supplied Simons three-view above, and [actual aircraft photographs posted by their photographer, described as Barre airport around 1986](https://www.rcuniverse.com/forum/rc-gliders-sailplanes-slope-soaring-112/1960701-schweizer-2-33-kit.html) were inspected. In particular, [attachment 202844](https://www.rcuniverse.com/forum/attachment.php?attachmentid=202844&stc=1) shows the thin upper sheet, four hinge-edge tabs, three intervening reliefs, pale outer paint and gray inner face. These are photographs of the full-size aircraft within an RC discussion, not photographs of an RC model. Photo rights are unverified; they remain external references and are not product textures.

Digitizing the three-view gives an approximate 45-inch panel span, starting about 75 inches from the aircraft centerline and ending at 120 inches. These are visual tracing estimates, not explicit factory dimensions. The upper panel occupies roughly 50–60 percent chord, aft of its hinge; the lower occupies roughly 40–50 percent chord, forward of its hinge. Each closed panel follows the existing NACA43012A wing skin and spanwise hinge, including dihedral and incidence. Three small hinge-edge reliefs are modeled from the photograph. Color and relief dimensions are reference choices, not an operator-specific fit.

The four old slabs are replaced in the generated Blender source, serialized static/moving meshes and both authored aircraft prefab bindings. Both upper and lower panels now rotate about their actual rightward hinge axes; because the lower panel extends forward, its rotation sign differs from the old aft-extending box. Animation continues to use the actual native JSBSim actuator position, including intermediate travel and retraction. There is no flight-model change.

The representation uses double-sided thin sheets and a shallow dark recess surface; it does not model an internal linkage or a through-wing cavity. Total SGS cost is 7,993 triangles, three renderers, two materials and zero textures (previously 7,921 triangles; the 8,000-triangle ceiling is unchanged). Exact acceptance and evidence paths are in `SGS_AIRBRAKE_STATUS.json`. The new assets are not installed on the headset.

## Combined cockpit brake handle correction, 2026-10-16

The [manufacturer manual](https://www.faasafety.gov/files/events/GL/GL09/2018/GL0983174/schweizer_2_33_manual.pdf) places the front dive-brake/wheel-brake handle on the left below the instrument panel. It describes pushing forward/down to unlock, pulling straight aft, and wheel braking at the extreme aft end. The [2017 RCAF aircraft operating instructions](https://bc-aircadetleague.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/403-manual-en-acgp-2-33-aois-2017.pdf), paragraph8, corroborate this sequence. These are aircraft references, not Mile High procedures.

The actual [SGS2-33/A seller cockpit photo](https://wingsandwheels.com/media/catalog/product/i/m/img_3171.jpeg), shown in this [aircraft listing](https://wingsandwheels.com/classifieds/gliders/schweizer-sgs-2-331962829070.html), shows a long sidewall shaft and bent inward-facing grip. An actual [N2930H cockpit photograph](https://forums.flightsimulator.com/uploads/default/original/4X/c/5/a/c5a92821d7efde1d78dbd8eac820d2a0bc7103f1.jpeg), posted in a simulator development discussion, corroborates the arrangement with different grip finish. The [AOPA photograph by David Tulis](https://www.aopa.org/news-and-media/all-news/2025/december/pilot/schweizer-elevated) shows a white shaft and black grip on a refurbished2-33B; it supports that finish choice only, not2-33A dimensions. All three images were inspected. Photo rights remain unverified; files stay external and are not textures.

The old blue upright rod/ball is removed from the generated source and both serialized aircraft presentations. Its replacement has a white fore-aft shaft, bent elbow, black inward grip and fixed guide sleeve. The closed grip is at(-0.21,0.20,0.94)m in the authored aircraft frame, below the panel edge. The existing trim handle is lowered110mm to preserve the trim-below-brake arrangement and separate the targets. Trim shape and input mapping remain unchanged. No camera, seat, flight model, exterior brake panel or scenery changes are part of this correction.

Handle motion uses a short downward roll followed by straight aft travel; its visible grip and SDK grab target follow the same pose. A new physical unlocking gesture/force detent is not modeled: the short visual roll happens automatically during the initial pull. The230mm stroke,25-degree roll during the first8percent, mounting coordinates and existing85/15 control split are defensible reference configuration estimates, not measured factory rigging. Exact operator configuration remains deferred.

The **same handle controls both airbrakes and the center wheel brake**. The existing production mapping is retained:0–85percent of travel extends the airbrakes from0–100percent;85–100percent keeps them fully deployed and increases wheel braking from0–100percent. Returning the handle releases the wheel brake before retracting the airbrakes. Tests exercise actual SDK grabs and regrabs in both directions, including partial wheel braking, and check the published flight inputs. The native bridge routes these independent channels to speedbrake and center-wheel brake commands; there is no Unity flight-dynamics substitute.

Geometric checks cover21brake positions,21trim positions and nine nominal shoulder variations. Brake reach stays below0.60m and brake/trim targets stay over0.10m apart. These checks are not worn comfort or controller-clearance acceptance. Both-eye offline closeups at closed,unlock,half,airbrake-full,wheel-brake-full and reclosed states were inspected. Fresh live XR and headset acceptance are deferred; no updated APK was installed. Current cost is **7,985triangles,3renderers,2materials,0textures**, eight triangles below the preceding exterior brake repair. See `SGS_BRAKE_LEVER_STATUS.json` for exact evidence and limits.

## Front panel and nose view correction, 2026-10-16

There is no universal exact instrument panel for all2-33s. The historical manufacturer manual specifies an ASI and permits additional instruments. The [RCAF2017 AOI](https://bc-aircadetleague.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/403-manual-en-acgp-2-33-aois-2017.pdf), paragraph12 and Figure1-3, documents altimeter, airspeed indicator, magnetic compass and either VSI or variometer; radios/transponders are optional. Its panel is explicitly an example. Figures1-2,1-3 and3-1 were rendered and inspected from the PDF. Operator registration, exact installed makes and measured mounting dimensions remain deferred.

The selected layout follows the actual [N2930H cockpit photograph](https://forums.flightsimulator.com/uploads/default/original/4X/c/5/a/c5a92821d7efde1d78dbd8eac820d2a0bc7103f1.jpeg): altimeter far left, pressure VSI at inner left, small magnetic compass low centre, centre fresh-air vent above it, MPH ASI inner right, and a blank outer-right cutout. This is a photographed reference arrangement, not an assertion that N2930H is the user's aircraft. The [Wings & Wheels actual cockpit photo](https://wingsandwheels.com/media/catalog/product/i/m/img_3171.jpeg) and [Stefan Trego's flying cockpit photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/stefantrego/6415291663/) corroborate the arched panel, coaming, vent, compass and simple four-instrument fit, with different instrument placements. The [Central Ohio Soaring Association front-seat tow photograph](https://centralohiosoaring.org/introductory-flight) was inspected for the forward sightline and raked pitot/static mast; its extra avionics are not copied into this selected fit.

The flat/open shelf ahead of the panel is replaced by a closed, curved ivory coaming joining the forward nose/canopy seam. The front glazing and seam follow that arch, the panel is widened from580 to650mm, and the missing raked pitot/static mast replaces the incorrectly placed nose-tip tube. The original overall fuselage envelope and lifting surfaces are retained apart from the forward seam fit. Dimensions, mast height and panel placement are visual estimates; the existing seat eye, camera ownership and all control anchors remain unchanged. The RCAF8.1-degree vision-over-nose criterion is an operator reference, not a measured acceptance result for this model or the user.

The former heading needle is removed. A vertical-axis compass card now turns behind a fixed viewing window and lubber line, using the same declared7.66-degree east Boulder magnetic variation as the C172 instruments. It does not simulate compass dip, acceleration errors or updated magnetic-field variation. The ASI is marked in MPH, with a photographed-style0–160mph face and matching piecewise needle angles; those angles are estimated from a photo, not a calibrated instrument survey. Native calibrated airspeed still supplies the indication; pneumatic position/error modeling is unchanged. The pressure VSI uses a left-zero face marked in thousands of feet per minute, with a selected±1500fpm range. The altimeter has separate hundreds,thousands and ten-thousands hands and retains the existing fixed standard-pressure indication, identified by STD. An adjustable Kollsman mechanism is not implemented by this visual task. No powered gyros, inactive radios, fabricated operator limits or copied registrations are added.

All indications continue to use the existing native JSBSim-backed snapshots. The mesh reauthoring helper now refreshes GPU buffers explicitly: inspection exposed stale static geometry in same-process previews even though the saved asset data had changed. Final captures use refreshed meshes, and a separate Unity session verifies the serialized assets. An attempted omission of forward inner skin exposed a footwell opening; it was rejected and that skin restored before final acceptance.

Resources remain under the existing ceiling: **7,991triangles,3renderers,2materials,0textures**. Adjacent glyph rectangles are merged, the redundant linear middle row of the coaming is omitted, and only the buried rear half of the trim bead is culled. The visible nose interior is retained. Research photos remain external and uncommitted, with no photo textures in the product. Exact checks, source/model hashes and deferred live XR/full APK/headset acceptance are recorded in `SGS_PANEL_VIEW_STATUS.json`.


## Trim lever and preflight correction, 2026-10-16

The [RCAF 2017 AOI](https://bc-aircadetleague.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/403-manual-en-acgp-2-33-aois-2017.pdf), section 1 paragraph 11, distinguishes floor bungee, left-wall bungee and stick-ratchet mechanisms. The selected reference remains the early **left-wall bungee trim below the spoiler handle, with four notches**. The [actual Wings & Wheels cockpit photograph](https://wingsandwheels.com/media/catalog/product/i/m/img_3171.jpeg) shows the dark flat bent arm, flattened round handgrip and notched sidewall slot. The photograph and rendered manual pages were inspected. N2930H's floor/stick installation is a different variant and was not used as the trim donor.

The former green ball/stalk is removed. The new arm, grip, notched quadrant and linkage are modeled directly into the existing static/moving meshes. The pivot is at (-0.310,-0.085,0.860)m and grip at (-0.275,0.070,0.860)m relative to aircraft CG. These are photographic/reach estimates, not surveyed dimensions. Existing signed continuous native trim, 155mm grip radius and +/-30-degree reference travel are retained, with the grab volume following the visible mesh. The physical latch, calibrated discrete bungee forces and four-position input snapping are **not** simulated; changing those would require a separate native flight-model calibration. No native model, force law, camera or seat calibration is changed.

The arbitrary trim-movement gate is removed from the simulated stopped-control checklist. This does not imply real pilots omit trim: section 3 paragraph 20 of the RCAF AOI explicitly lists a forward trim setting before takeoff, while the historical [manufacturer scan](https://www.faasafety.gov/files/events/GL/GL09/2018/GL0983174/schweizer_2_33_manual.pdf) recommends forward bungee trim for solo aerotow. The reference briefing explains this distinction and the simulator's calibrated preset. The historical scan disclaims use as a current aircraft operating document. No Mile High procedure or universal certified checklist is asserted.

Menu > Launch preparation now offers **Skip preflight and prepare launch**. This simulator convenience requires a valid stopped aircraft, reuses native crew restaging/reconnection, leaves traffic/brake/control launch gates intact, and records skipped status instead of manufacturing observed checks. Lessons advance past preparation without counting the skipped checklist skill or granting independent completion. An explicit restart begins a new unskipped session.

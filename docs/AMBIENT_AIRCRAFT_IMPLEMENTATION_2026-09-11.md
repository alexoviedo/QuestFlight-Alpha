Status: **Quest candidate built and installed; final worn acceptance pending**. Approved September 11, 2026, on `feature/boulder-ambient-aircraft-wind`. Unity licensing is restored. Preserve the accepted scenery/High FFR milestone and the user's local `DevAgentSettings.asset` edit.

## Implemented scope

Five persistent, Editor-authored actors: two C172s, one PA-18 Super Cub and two Discus CS-inspired sailplanes. There is no runtime aircraft/XR/environment hierarchy construction and no additional player flight authority. Three exterior LODs share one opaque vertex-color material without realtime shadow passes. Propeller and flap pivots are animated. The Cub has a tail-down ground pose and raises its tail during acceleration. Sailplanes remain airborne; no fictitious self-taxi, disconnected tow or powered go-around is added.

Powered airport movements run one complete cycle at a time, rotating among the three powered slots. This deliberately quiet schedule replaced opposing taxi flows that caused repeated go-arounds. Two sailplanes operate independently. Six terrain-grounded routes cover both runway directions, apron movement, hold-short, lineup, takeoff, climb, north circuit, approach, flare, rollout, taxi-in and shutdown. The east taxi connection follows the inspected orthophoto at approximately ENU x=550 m. Player protection covers both strips and unexpected opposite-direction finals. Predictive conflict checks, ground braking, bounded escape turns, offscreen retirement and swept last-resort intersection suppression are distinct behaviors. Normal acceptance requires zero emergency suppressions.

A shared deterministic 161 x 161 wind grid supplies native JSBSim, traffic, the windsock and the eagle's wind-relative heading/updraft cue. Calm preserves the accepted airport thermal exactly. Foothill soaring adds moderate easterly flow, smooth bounded gusts, signed terrain lift/lee sink and a tilted foothill thermal. The eight existing cloud volumes drift together with sampled horizontal wind; density, geometry, culling and interior sampling share their existing transform. No cloud samples, passes or volumes are added. Clouds are not recycled or teleported; long-session coverage after sustained drift remains a later acceptance check. The eagle retains its authored encounter path and animation envelope, not a new avian flight model.

Native ENU-to-NED mapping is `(east, up, north) -> (north, east, -up)`. Nonfinite/out-of-envelope wind fails closed. Weather selection is restricted to the paused startup selection state. Native JSBSim remains the sole player flight authority; no camera, calibration or pilot-pose intervention is used for wind or avoidance.
